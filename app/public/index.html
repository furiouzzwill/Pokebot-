      Discovery settings

      A find must match one of these. Comma separated.

      Rejected even if a keyword matched. Keywords stay loose enough to catch a set nobody has heard of yet; this is what keeps the socks out.

      Without the r/. Comma separated.

      Auto-add puts a found URL straight into the watchlist, where your live settings apply to it. Leave it off unless you trust your keywords.

      Weekday names, comma separated.

      An IANA name, not EST. America/New_York is Eastern all year and moves itself for daylight saving.

      Walmart or Target search, browse or category URLs, comma separated. Sort by newest. Opened when the window opens, closed when it shuts.

      A drop page usually doesn't exist until minutes beforehand, so keep a Walmart or Target category tab open — sorted newest first, not a plain search. Relevance ranking buries a brand new SKU. Below 5s the re-query stops looking like someone with a tab open, and a bot check during the one minute that matters costs you the drop.
